## Extracted Content
Databricks is a company that has revolutionized data management by creating a 'lake house' architecture.

*   **Problem:** Companies traditionally used either expensive, rigid data warehouses for structured data or cheap, flexible but unreliable data lakes for unstructured data, often managing both separately.
*   **Solution:** Databricks built upon Apache Spark, an open-source technology for processing massive datasets, to create a hybrid 'lake house' that offers the speed and reliability of data warehouses with the flexibility and cost-effectiveness of data lakes.
*   **Business Model:** By open-sourcing Spark and the lake house concept, Databricks fostered widespread adoption. They then built proprietary features and a managed platform service on this open-source foundation, allowing customers to leverage their data infrastructure for AI workloads and pay Databricks as a utility.

Then they made a strange decision. They open source both Apache Spark and the lake house, giving it away entirely for free. They bet that if the world built on top of their technology, the adoption would create a moat. It totally worked. Over the next decade, millions of developers across thousands of big companies built their entire data infrastructure on Spark.
