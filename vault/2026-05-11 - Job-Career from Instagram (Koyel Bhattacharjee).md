### Key Takeaways on [[LinkedIn]] Usage

**Actionable Advice:**
*   **[[LinkedIn]] as a Support Tool:** [[LinkedIn]] is highly useful, but primarily as a supportive extension of your [[CV]] or resume, not a direct interview generator on its own.
*   **Profile Strength Matters:** A strong [[LinkedIn]] profile significantly increases your chances of being noticed by recruiters, while a blank profile can actively hurt your prospects.
*   **Consistency is Crucial:** Your [[LinkedIn]] profile and [[CV]] must be consistent and identical in their core information. Recruiters will check both, and discrepancies are detrimental.
*   **Extend, Don't Duplicate:** Think of your [[LinkedIn]] profile as an *extended version* of your [[CV]], offering more detail and context than your resume can typically hold.
*   **Synergistic Approach:** The real power comes from combining a strong [[CV]] with a well-maintained, consistent [[LinkedIn]] profile. Neither is magic alone, but together they are very effective.
