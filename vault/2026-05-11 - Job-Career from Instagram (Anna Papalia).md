## Things to Avoid in a [[Job Interview]]

### [[Key Skills]] Emphasized:
*   [[Self-awareness]]: Knowing your strengths, weaknesses, and career goals.
*   [[Preparation]]: Researching the company and practicing answers to common questions.
*   [[Communication Skills]]: Clearly articulating your value and engaging with the interviewer.
*   [[Proactiveness]]: Asking thoughtful questions and demonstrating genuine interest.

### [[Actionable Advice]]:
*   **Prepare for Basic Questions**: Always be ready to answer fundamental questions like "[[Tell me about yourself]]", "[[Where do you see yourself in the next five years?]]", and "[[What are your strengths and weaknesses?]]". These are expected.
*   **Articulate Your Value to the Company**: When asked "[[Why do you want to work here?]]", focus on what *you* can do for the company, not just the benefits or what the company can do for you. Demonstrate how your skills and experience align with their needs.
*   **Always Have Questions**: When the interviewer asks, "[[Do you have any questions for me?]]", always have thoughtful questions prepared. Saying "No, I'm good" conveys a lack of interest or engagement.
*   **Know Why You Should Be Hired**: Be prepared to answer "[[Why should I hire you?]]" with a clear, concise, and compelling argument that highlights your unique qualifications and how they will benefit the company.
