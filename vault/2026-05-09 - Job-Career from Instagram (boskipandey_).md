### Actionable Advice:
*   **[[Zintellect]]** (zintellect.com):
    *   **Purpose**: The official [[government portal]] for [[national lab]] and [[research internships]], including programs from [[NASA]], [[DOE]], and [[ORISE]].
    *   **Action**: Apply here for [[high-level research opportunities]] that most [[students]] overlook.
*   **[[Interstride]]** (interstride.com):
    *   **Purpose**: Built exclusively for [[international students]].
    *   **Action**: Use its filters to find [[roles with visa sponsorship]], avoiding wasted applications.
*   **[[Untapped]]** (untapped.io):
    *   **Purpose**: [[Recruiters]] actively search for [[candidates]] based on [[profiles]].
    *   **Action**: Create a [[profile]]; [[recruiters]] will find you. Look for [[roles]] clearly flagged for [[visa sponsorship]] among over 40,000 [[listings]].
*   **[[Parker Dewey]]** (parkerdewey.com):
    *   **Purpose**: Offers paid [[micro-internships]].
    *   **Action**: Start here if you can't land a [[full internship]]. These [[short-term projects]] often convert into [[full offers]], and [[no experience]] is required.
*   **[[Extern]]** (extern.com):
    *   **Purpose**: Provides real [[projects]] from [[Fortune 1000]] [[companies]], fully [[remote]].
    *   **Action**: Ideal [[entry point]] for [[first-year students]] as it has no [[GPA cutoff]] or [[major requirement]].
*   **[[GitHub List]]** (github.com - search "Summer 2026 Internships"):
    *   **Purpose**: A [[crowdsourced]], [[real-time list]] of open [[tech internships]].
    *   **Action**: Monitor this [[list]] frequently, as some [[roles]] are posted here before official [[company websites]].
*   **[[WayUp]]** (wayup.com):
    *   **Purpose**: A [[platform]] exclusively for [[students]].
    *   **Action**: Create a [[profile]] to allow [[employers]] to browse and reach out directly, especially if you're not at a [[target recruiting school]].
*   **[[Chegg Internships]]** (internships.chegg.com):
    *   **Purpose**: [[Listings]] filtered specifically for [[students]], not [[general job boards]].
    *   **Action**: Use this for your [[first internship search]], leveraging its built-in [[resume guidance]] and [[interview prep resources]].
