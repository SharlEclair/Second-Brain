* Dinner for Dummies Day 27: Dump & Bake Tandoori Chicken and Rice 🍚 

One pan, zero fuss - spiced tandoori chicken baked over fluffy tandoori rice.

Ingredients
5-8 x Chicken Thighs
20g x Tandoori Spice Rub
1 1/2 cup x Rice (washed) 
3 x Tbs Tandoori Pasta
400mL Can x Coconut Milk
1 Cup x Chicken Stock
1 x Onion, thinly sliced
Greek yoghurt 
Cucumber
Lemon 
Garlic Powder 

Method:
1. Preheat your oven to 200°C (180°C fan-forced).
2. In a large baking dish, combine rice, tandoori paste, coconut milk, chicken stock, and onion. 
3. Stir well so the tandoori paste is evenly mixed through and the rice is fully submerged.
4. Place the chicken thighs skin side up directly on top of the rice. 
5. Shake tandoori spice rub generously over each piece of chicken. Drizzle with oil. 
6. Cover tightly with foil - make sure there are no gaps or the rice won’t cook properly.
7. Bake for 45 minutes until the rice has absorbed the liquid and the chicken is cooked through. (if rice isn’t cooked you can add more stock and cook for longer). 
8. Remove the foil and switch to grill and bake uncovered for 10–15 minutes. 
9. Mix yoghurt, cucumber, salt, garlic powder and lemon to make a quick sauce. 
10. Dollop over the dish, top with coriander and a squeeze of lemon and serve.
