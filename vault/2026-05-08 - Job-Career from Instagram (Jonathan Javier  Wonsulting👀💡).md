### Actionable [[Job Search Advice]]

*   **Avoid traditional platforms:** Don't rely solely on [[LinkedIn]] or [[Indeed]], as they often contain reposted or old [[job listings]] from companies not actively hiring.
*   **Explore alternative sites:** Utilize less-known, highly effective platforms that offer current [[job openings]] and advanced features.

### Recommended Platforms & How to Use Them

1.  **[[Google Jobs]]**:
    *   **How to use:** Search your desired [[role]] directly on [[Google]].
    *   **Benefits:** Finds tailored [[jobs]], displays [[qualifications]], [[compensation]], and [[benefits]] (e.g., [[salary]], [[benefits]]).

2.  **[[Job Board AI]]**:
    *   **How to use:** Identifies [[jobs]] where you meet at least 80% of the [[qualifications]].
    *   **Benefits:** Offers [[auto-apply features]] and free [[resume]]/[[cover letter]] tailoring.

3.  **[[Job Today]]**:
    *   **How to use:** Search by [[location]] (e.g., [[NYC]]) and specific [[position]].
    *   **Benefits:** Finds both [[full-time]] and [[part-time jobs]] in your [[local area]].
