# Recipe by Fallow

> **AI Summary:** This recipe details how to make Swedish meatballs and accompaniments like pomme puree, pickled cucumbers, and cream gravy, aiming for a Michelin-star quality.

**Steps:**

1.  **Meatball Mixture:** Combine beef and pork mince, oil, pepper, allspice, egg, Dijon mustard, milk and cream, breadcrumbs, and salt. Mix well while keeping the mix cold.
2.  **Form Meatballs:** Roll the mixture into 45g balls.
3.  **Pomme Puree (Mashed Potatoes):
    *   Salt the bottom of a baking tray and place potatoes on top.
    *   Bake at 180°C.
    *   Once cooked, make holes in the potatoes.
    *   Halve the cooked potatoes and push them through a drum sieve.
    *   Dehydrate and roast the potato mash slightly in the oven.
    *   In a saucepan, heat equal quantities of milk and cream. Add potato skins and a couple of pinches of Maldon salt. Bring to a simmer.
    *   Gradually add the warm potato mixture to the sieved potato, incorporating butter and salt until smooth.
4.  **Pickled Cucumbers:**
    *   Combine vinegar, sugar, water, yellow mustard seeds, and a tiny bit of Maldon salt in a pan.
    *   Pour the pickle mixture over sliced cucumbers and let sit.
5.  **Cream Sauce:**
    *   While cooking meatballs, make a sauce from the pan's residual fond.
    *   Infuse cream by heating it to 80°C with peppercorns, bay leaf, thyme, and rosemary. Strain the cream.
    *   Soften finely chopped shallots and garlic in the pan.
    *   Add about a liter of stock and the infused cream.
    *   Reduce the sauce, adding Dijon mustard towards the end.
6.  **Cranberry Sauce:**
    *   Combine cranberries, sugar, a good pinch of salt, and some lemon juice in a pan.
    *   Cook until the sugar starts to caramelize and the sauce reduces.
7.  **Cook Meatballs:** Season a pan and cook the meatballs until browned and cooked through, basting with pan juices.
8.  **Assembly:** Pile mash potatoes onto a plate, add meatballs, top with cranberry jam, dill pickles, and finish with the cream gravy.
