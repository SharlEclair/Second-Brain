# Recipe by Easy Chicken Recipes

> **AI Summary:** A recipe for Grilled Parmesan Chicken featuring a homemade parmesan seasoning and melted camembert cheese, perfect for dinner.

## Extracted Content
- [ ] 1000 g boneless, skinless chicken thighs
- [ ] 2 tbsp homemade parmesan seasoning
- [ ] 1 tbsp oil
- [ ] Camembert (sliced)

**Homemade Parmesan Seasoning:**
- [ ] 1 tbsp smoked paprika
- [ ] 1 tbsp garlic powder
- [ ] 1 tbsp onion powder
- [ ] 1 tsp black pepper
- [ ] 1 tsp salt
- [ ] 1 tsp brown sugar
- [ ] 1 tsp chili flakes or cayenne (optional)
- [ ] 1 tsp dried thyme or rosemary (optional)
- [ ] A generous handful of freshly grated parmesan

**Cook it:**
1. Rub the chicken with the seasoning mix + oil.
2. Let it marinate for at least 30 min – longer = more flavor.
3. Get the pan ripping hot with a little oil.
4. Sear 3–4 min per side until deep golden and cooked through.
5. In the last minute – crust it with extra parmesan straight in the pan and let it melt & crisp up.
6. Lay slices of camembert right on top of the hot chicken.
7. Cover the pan and let it melt until gooey and golden.

**Serve it:**
- Pile it over fluffy jasmine or basmati rice.
- Let that melted camembert drip all over everything.
- Finish with more parmesan on top.
