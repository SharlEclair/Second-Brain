* My take on Tangra-style chilli chicken… with a twist
Crispy. Saucy. Addictive.🌶️🔥
Save this before you forget.

Marination (mix everything well):
Bite-sized chicken + salt + black pepper + a pinch of baking soda (for tenderness) + egg white (for coating) + chilli flakes + a little soy sauce
👉 Rest for 30–60 mins

⸻

Coating (for crunch):
Add maida + cornflour + rice flour directly to the marinated chicken
👉 Mix till it forms a light, sticky coating (not watery)

⸻

Sauces (mix separately):
Dark soy sauce + fish sauce + tomato ketchup + green chilli sauce + hot sauce + a little vinegar + salt + pepper + MSG

⸻

Method:

1. Heat oil and fry chicken pieces on medium heat till cooked
2. Take out, rest 2–3 mins, then fry again on high heat till golden & crispy (double fry = extra crunch)
3. In another pan, heat a little oil
  Add dried red chillies + finely chopped ginger & garlic
  Add onions + bell peppers → stir fry on high flame
4. Add fried chicken + prepared sauces
 👉 Toss everything on high flame for 30–40 seconds (don’t overcook or it loses crunch)
