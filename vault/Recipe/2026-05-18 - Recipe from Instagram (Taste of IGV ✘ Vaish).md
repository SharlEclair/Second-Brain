**Steps:**
1. Prepare the marinade by combining all marinade ingredients.
2. Marinate the chicken pieces.
3. Cook chicken in the air fryer at 190 degrees Celsius for 15 minutes, skin side up.
4. While chicken cooks, prepare the mint and coriander sauce.
5. Flip chicken and cook for another 10 minutes at 180 degrees Celsius.
