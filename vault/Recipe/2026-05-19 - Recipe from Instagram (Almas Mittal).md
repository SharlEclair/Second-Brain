# Recipe by Almas Mittal

> **AI Summary:** A classic Indochinese Chilli Chicken recipe made in an air fryer, perfect as a snack or served with rice.

## Extracted Content
- [ ] 1/2 kg boneless cubed chicken thighs
- [ ] 1 egg white
- [ ] 1 tbsp soy sauce
- [ ] 1 tbsp ginger garlic paste
- [ ] Salt, Pepper, Paprika
- [ ] 1 tbsp cornflour
- [ ] 1 tbsp maida

**Sauce:**
- [ ] 1/2 cup whites of green onions
- [ ] 1 tbsp chopped garlic 
- [ ] 1 tbsp chopped ginger
- [ ] 1/2 cup Green bell pepper
- [ ] 1/2 cup red bell pepper
- [ ] 2 tbsp Soy sauce
- [ ] 1 tbsp red chilli sauce 
- [ ] 1 tbsp green chilli sauce 
- [ ] 1 tbsp sweet chilli sauce 
- [ ] 1/2 tbsp rice vinegar 
- [ ] Salt
- [ ] Spring onions to garnish

**Instructions:**
1. Marinate the chicken with egg white, soy sauce, ginger garlic paste, salt, pepper, and paprika. Mix well, then add cornflour and maida and shake.
2. Air fry at 200°C for 10-15 minutes, flipping halfway.
3. To a pan, add whites of spring onion, ginger, garlic, and bell peppers. Since using less oil, add a little water and cover to steam and cook the peppers.
4. Add soy sauce, red chilli sauce, green chilli sauce, rice vinegar, and sweet chilli sauce. Mix well.
5. Add the cooked chicken pieces and mix well to coat them with the sauce. A cornstarch slurry can be added for a thicker, glossy sauce.
6. Garnish with spring onions and enjoy.
