Steps:
1. Marinate chicken in salt, chili sauce, garlic powder, and buttermilk for at least 2 hours in the fridge.
2. Prepare the coating: Mix two parts plain flour, one part cornflour, baking powder, and KFC's 11 secret herbs and spices.
3. Prepare the wet batter: Mix equal parts plain flour and cornstarch, special seasoning, two semi-beaten egg whites, a tiny bit of vodka, and thin with water until you have a nice thin batter.
4. Coat the chicken: Remove chicken from buttermilk, let excess drip off, coat evenly in the dry mix.
5. Dip the chicken into the wet batter.
6. Re-coat the chicken in the dry mix for a final coating.
7. Fry the coated chicken in hot oil at 180°C.
