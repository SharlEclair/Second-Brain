### Original Caption
* spicy penne pasta 🍝🌶️

ingredients:

1.5 cups penne pasta
2 tbsp oil
2 tbsp butter
1/2 onion, chopped
2 tbsp garlic, chopped
4 tbsp tomato paste
salt to taste 
1 tsp red chilli flakes
1/2 tsp garlic powder
1 tsp oregano
1 tsp black pepper powder
1/2 tsp cayenne pepper or red chilli powder
1/3rd cup heavy cream
1 cup reserved pasta water 
dried/fresh parsley
shredded mozzarella cheese (as much as you want) 

recipe:

1. boil pasta and save some of the pasta water.

2. in a pan, warm up 2 tbsp oil and add half an onion chopped, along with chopped garlic. sauté for a few minutes.

3. add 4 tbsp tomato paste and cook for two minutes. then add salt, red chilli flakes, garlic powder, oregano, black pepper powder, and cayenne pepper or red chilli powder. mix well.

4. add heavy cream along with some reserved pasta water and mix well. let it cook for 2 minutes on a medium flame. add butter and let it incorporate into the sauce 

5. add the boiled pasta and mix well. add mozzarella cheese and optionally add red chilli flakes, fresh or dried parsley and italian seasoning on top. and enjoy!
