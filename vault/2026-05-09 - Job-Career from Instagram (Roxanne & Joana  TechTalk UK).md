## Preparing for the Recruiter Screening Call

This advice comes from Joanna, a [[Hiring Manager]] and [[Career Coach]] who has helped over 400 people get hired. She emphasizes that preparing for each [[interview stage]] differently is crucial for success, as [[Recruiters]] and [[Hiring Managers]] look for distinct things.

### Understanding the Recruiter's Role
[[Recruiters]] typically assess candidates at a basic level, primarily checking if your profile aligns with a specific [[checklist]] derived from the [[job description]]. They often don't delve into the technical depth of the role.

### Actionable Advice for Your Next Screening Call:

1.  **Prepare for Common Interview Questions**:
    *   "Tell me about yourself."
    *   "What are your [[salary expectations]]?"
    *   "Why are you leaving your current company?" or "Why do you want to join this company?"
    *   *Tip*: Have strong, well-rehearsed answers ready as this is your crucial first impression.

2.  **Conduct a [[Gap Analysis]] of the Job Description**:
    *   **Step 1**: Copy the "what they're looking for in a candidate" section from the [[job description]] into a [[Google Document]].
    *   **Step 2**: Break down each point and [[color-code]] it:
        *   **Green**: Skills/experiences you *have* done.
        *   **Yellow**: Skills/experiences you have *partially* done.
        *   **Red**: Skills/experiences you *haven't* done.
    *   **Step 3**: For *every* point (including red ones), prepare a concrete example. For "red" items, think of related experiences that are very close to what they're looking for. This ensures you have examples ready for potential questions.

3.  **Prepare Questions to Ask the Recruiter**:
    *   Focus on the [[interview process]], the [[team]], and your [[candidate profile]].
    *   Example questions:
        *   "What does the [[interview process]] look like from here?"
        *   "What is the [[team]] looking for in an ideal candidate?"
        *   "Based on our conversation and my [[CV]], do you see any gaps that would prevent me from going to the next stage?"

4.  **Practice Out Loud**:
    *   Rehearse your answers and questions aloud to build confidence and refine your delivery.

### Key Takeaways:
*   Different [[interview stages]] require different preparation strategies.
*   [[Recruiters]] use the [[job description]] as a [[checklist]] to screen candidates.
*   A thorough [[gap analysis]] helps anticipate questions and prepare relevant examples.
*   Proactively asking insightful questions demonstrates engagement and helps clarify expectations.
