### Original Caption
* Bang Bang Chicken Popcorn

Ingredients
The Sesame Buttermilk Marinade
500g Chicken breast (cut into small popcorn-sized cubes)
½ cup Buttermilk
1 tbsp Sesame seeds
The Spices: ½ tsp Salt, ½ tsp Black pepper, ½ tsp Chilli powder, ½ tsp Garlic powder, ½ tsp Onion powder, and a pinch of MSG.

The Crispy Coating
1 cup All-purpose flour
¼ cup Cornflour (cornstarch)
The Spices: ½ tsp Salt, ½ tsp Black pepper, ½ tsp Chilli powder, ½ tsp Onion powder.

The Bang Bang Sauce
½ cup Mayonnaise
1 tbsp Red chili sauce (like Sriracha or sweet chili)
1 tsp Chili oil (with flakes)
1 tsp Lemon juice
A pinch of salt

Instructions
1. Marinate for Tenderness
In a large bowl, whisk together the buttermilk, sesame seeds, and all the spices (salt, pepper, chilli, garlic, onion powder, and MSG). Add your chicken cubes and toss until they are fully submerged. Let this marinate for at least 30 minutes—the buttermilk tenderizes the chicken while the sesame seeds stick to the surface.

2. The Flour Crunch
In a separate shallow bowl, whisk together the flour, cornflour, and the coating spices.

Tip: Lift the chicken pieces directly from the buttermilk and drop them into the flour. Toss them vigorously so the flour clumps slightly around the wet chicken—this creates those extra-crispy “crags.”

3. Deep Fry to Gold
Heat oil in a deep pan over medium-high heat. Fry the chicken popcorn in batches for 4–5 minutes until they are a deep golden brown and sound “hollow” when tapped with a spoon. Drain them on a wire rack or paper towel.

4. Whisk the Bang Bang Sauce
While the chicken is frying, combine the mayonnaise, red chili sauce, chili oil, and lemon juice in a large mixing bowl. Stir until you have a creamy, vibrant orange sauce.

5. The Final Toss
Add the hot, crispy chicken popcorn directly into the sauce bowl. Toss quickly and gently until every piece is glazed in the Bang Bang Sauce.

6. Serving
Serve immediately while the coating is still crunchy. You can garnish with extra sesame seeds or chopped spring onions for a professional touch.
