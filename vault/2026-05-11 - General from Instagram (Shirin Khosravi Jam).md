## Extracted Content
Understanding the difference between [[Skill.md]] files and [[Plugins]] can be confusing at first.

A [[Skill.md]] file represents a skill you already know; it's a markdown file with instructions to perform a specific task and lives in your `.cloud` folder. In contrast, a [[Plugin]] is a bundled package layered on top, encompassing [[skills]], [[agents]], [[hooks]], and even [[MCP servers]] all in one installable, versioned item.

Once a [[plugin]] is installed, you gain access to all its components – the [[skills]], the [[agents]], and any necessary [[MCP servers]].

**When to use what:**
*   **[[Skill.md]]**: Ideal if you are building a [[personal workflow]] or managing a single project.
*   **[[Plugin]]**: Needed if you are sharing functionalities across your team, your organization, or distributing through a marketplace.

### Summary:
*   [[Skill.md]] files are single markdown files containing instructions for specific tasks, stored in your `.cloud` folder, best for personal or single-project use.
*   [[Plugins]] are comprehensive, versioned packages that bundle multiple [[skills]], [[agents]], [[hooks]], and [[MCP servers]] for broader installation and sharing.
*   Utilize [[Skill.md]] for individual workflows, and [[Plugins]] for collaborative team environments or market distribution.
