## How to Fix Your [[LinkedIn]] [[Profile]]

### [[Positioning]]

*   **Your [[Banner]]**: This is the [[first impression]]. Design it to direct [[traffic]] effectively.
*   **Your [[Headline]]**: Clearly and credibly position yourself, explaining the [[value]] you provide.
    *   **[[Headline Formula]]**: I help [[Specific audience]] Achieve [[Specific Outcome]] Through [[Unique Approach]]

### [[About Section]]

*   **Your [[Bio]]**: After the headline, focus less on mere [[credentials]] and more on showcasing your [[unique character]] and the [[transformation]] you can offer.
    *   Explain *why* you are the right person to help others achieve their [[objectives]].
    *   Narrate your [[journey]] and key [[achievements]], demonstrating [[growth]] and [[impact]] (e.g., starting [[businesses]], achieving [[revenue milestones]] like [[8 figures]]).

### Use Your [[Authority]]

Build [[authority]] through your [[content creation]] by demonstrating:

*   **[[Experience]]**: What you've done, [[businesses built]], [[results delivered]], [[years in the industry]].
*   **[[Expertise]]**: [[Unique knowledge]], [[frameworks]], and hard-won [[insights]].
*   **[[Evidence]]**: [[Proof]] of your [[approach]]'s [[effectiveness]] through [[case studies]], [[testimonials]], and receipts.
*   *Note*: If you lack [[traditional experience]], leverage that [[perspective]] as a [[unique angle]].

### [[Featured Section]]

Showcase your [[offers]] and [[key content]] here. Use the “link” option to create [[custom graphics]] ([[1200 x 627 Pixels]]).

*   **[[Free Offer]]**: Link to a [[newsletter]] or [[valuable resource]] to capture [[middle-of-funnel audiences]].
*   **[[Paid Offer]]**: Link to your [[company]] or [[services]] for those ready to [[purchase]] or [[trial]].
*   *Recommendation*: Keep this section to two or three [[key offers]].

### [[Experience Section]]

There's no single [[magic formula]], but aim to summarize your [[career journey]] and [[accomplishments]].

*   **[[Checklist]] for [[Experience Entries]]**:
    *   Show your [[achievements]] and the [[impact]] you made.
    *   Keep [[descriptions]] [[concise]] and to the point.
    *   Add relevant links to [[landing pages]] or [[lead magnets]] where appropriate.
