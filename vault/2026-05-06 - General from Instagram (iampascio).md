## Build a [[Second Brain]] in [[Notion]] (in 10 minutes)

Many people hesitate to build a [[personal knowledge management system]], thinking it's a monumental task. In reality, a functional "[[second brain]]" can be set up in [[Notion]] in just 10 minutes, offering a [[system]] you can use forever. The core idea is to free your main [[brain]] for thinking, not storing. Every idea you try to remember occupies [[mental space]]; a [[second brain]] captures everything so you can forget nothing, keeping [[notes]], [[projects]], [[ideas]], and [[resources]] in one always-findable place.

### The 4 Essential [[Database]]s

This [[system]] relies on only four core [[database]]s:

*   **[[Inbox]]**: For capturing everything fast.
*   **[[Projects]]**: For active work with deadlines.
*   **[[Areas]]**: For ongoing responsibilities without a finish line.
*   **[[Archive]]**: For anything done or inactive.

### Step-by-Step Setup

#### Step 1: Build your [[Inbox]]

1.  Create a new [[Notion page]] and turn it into a [[database]].
2.  Name it `[[Inbox]]`.
3.  Add one [[property]]: `[[Status]]` with options: `New`, `Processed`.
4.  This is where every new [[idea]], [[note]], [[link]], or [[thought]] goes first. The goal here is pure capture, no sorting or thinking.

#### Step 2: Build your [[Projects database]]

1.  Create a new [[database]] and name it `[[Projects]]`.
2.  Add three [[property]]s:
    *   `[[Status]]`: Options like `Not started`, `In progress`, `Done`.
    *   `[[Deadline]]`: A `[[Date]]` [[property]].
    *   `[[Area]]`: This will be a `[[Relation]]` [[property]], linked in Step 5.
3.  This [[database]] will house all your active work that has a [[deadline]].

#### Step 3: Build your [[Areas database]]

1.  Create a new [[database]] and name it `[[Areas]]`.
2.  [[Areas]] represent ongoing parts of your life that have no [[deadline]] or finish line (e.g., [[Health]], [[Finance]], [[Work]], [[Learning]]).
3.  Add a `[[Status]]` [[property]] with options: `Active` or `Paused`.

#### Step 4: Build your [[Archive]]

1.  Create a single [[database]] for everything inactive and name it `[[Archive]]`.
2.  This is where finished [[projects]], old [[notes]], and [[ideas]] you're not pursuing currently go.
3.  It keeps items out of your way but still searchable and never lost.

#### Step 5: Link everything together

1.  Go to your `[[Projects]]` [[database]].
2.  Add a `[[Relation]]` [[property]] and connect it to your `[[Areas]]` [[database]]. Now, every [[project]] can be associated with an [[area]] of your life.
3.  Do the same for your `[[Inbox]]` [[database]]: Add a `[[Relation]]` [[property]] and connect it to `[[Projects]]`. This allows you to easily move captured items into active [[projects]].

Your [[system]] is now a single, connected [[brain]].

### How Your [[Second Brain]] Works

1.  An [[idea]] hits.
2.  Drop it in your `[[Inbox]]`.
3.  When ready to act, move it to `[[Projects]]`.
4.  Link the [[project]] to an `[[Area]]`.
5.  Finish the [[project]].
6.  Send it to `[[Archive]]`.

This [[workflow]] ensures nothing falls through the cracks and prevents [[mental clutter]], allowing your [[second brain]] to run itself.
