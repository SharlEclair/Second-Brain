## Finding Your First Job in Australia as an International Student

This guide highlights industries that are frequently hiring casual staff and are good starting points for international students in Australia:

### 1. Supermarkets
- **Examples:** [[Coles]], [[Woolworths]]
- **Typical Roles:** Checkout operator, night fill, store assistant.
- **Benefit:** Offers stable part-time hours.

### 2. Fast Food Chains
- **Examples:** [[McDonald's Australia]], [[Hungry Jack's]], [[KFC Australia]]
- **Benefit:** Flexible shifts, accustomed to hiring individuals with little to no experience. Easier for under 18s.

### 3. Cafés & Restaurants
- **Reasoning:** High staff turnover leads to frequent openings.
- **Common Roles:** Kitchen hand, waiter, barista, dishwasher.
- **Tip:** Walking in with a [[Resume]] can be effective.

### 4. Warehouses
- **Roles:** Picker packer, sorting parcels, loading stock.
- **How to Find:** Often through labour hire agencies.

### 5. Cleaning Jobs
- **Sectors:** Offices, shopping centres, hotels.
- **Benefit:** Often evening shifts, suitable for students.

**Actionable Advice:**
- Your first job in [[Australia]] might not be your dream job, but it's crucial for gaining local work experience.
- Local work experience significantly eases the process of finding subsequent jobs.
- Always carry your [[Resume]] when applying for roles in hospitality.
