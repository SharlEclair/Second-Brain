### How to write effective [[Cold Email]]s for [[Job Opportunities]] & [[Internships]]

**Actionable Advice:**

1.  **Identify Shared Identities**: Look for individuals who share a common background (e.g., [[alumni]], women, specific ethnicity) to establish an initial connection.
2.  **Craft a Concise Email (3 Key Elements)**:
    *   **Who you are & your qualifications**: Provide a very short, bulleted list.
    *   **Value you can provide**: Focus on how you can directly benefit the company or individual (e.g., building a [[pitch deck]], coding solutions, etc.). Emphasize direct application of skills over general qualifications.
    *   **Clear & Easy Ask**: Make it simple for them to say yes. Options include:
        *   A 15-minute chat.
        *   A quick [[resume review]].
        *   **Offer specific availability**: Provide days/times you are free throughout the week.
        *   **Offer format options**: Ask if they prefer a virtual chat or a typed conversation.
