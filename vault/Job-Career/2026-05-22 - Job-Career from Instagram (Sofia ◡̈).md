## Unconventional Networking in Your 20s: A Guide

**Key Skills:**
- [[Authentic Interest]] in strangers
- [[Confidence]] in engaging with others
- [[Conversation Skills]] to build rapport
- [[Relationship Building]] for long-term connections

**Actionable Advice:**

1.  **Answer their question and give them what they want:** Start by addressing their immediate query or topic of interest.
2.  **Mirror ball or value bomb them:**
    *   **Validate:** Agree with their statements to create comfort (e.g., "Oh my gosh, it's freezing today. I completely agree!").
    *   **Don't deflect compliments:** Overexplain to create comfort (e.g., "Thank you so much, I just swear by these pants, they hug my waist so well") or add value (e.g., "These pants are from Skims, but I think you can definitely get some good dupes at Cotton On").
3.  **Pass the mic to them:** Introduce yourself and provide context, then ask open-ended questions like "What brings you here today?" or "Are you from around here? Are you just visiting?"
4.  **Instigate their purpose nonchalantly:** After some natural back-and-forth, ask "What do you do?" This creates an open end to question about their life, not just career, often leading to valuable contacts or opportunities.
