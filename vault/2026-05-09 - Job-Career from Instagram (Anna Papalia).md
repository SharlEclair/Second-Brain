### Interview Success: The Scarcity Principle Technique

This technique claims a 100% success rate in job interviews, including admissions for an [[Ivy League]] school. The core idea is to shift the dynamic from being interviewed to being pitched by the employer.

#### Key Skills:
*   **Confidence & Poise**: Presenting yourself as a highly sought-after candidate.
*   **Strategic Mindset**: Understanding and applying psychological principles like the [[scarcity principle]].
*   **Preparation**: Essential foundational work before the interview.
*   **Negotiation**: Crucial for securing the best possible offer.

#### Actionable Advice:
*   **Foundational Steps (Prerequisites)**:
    *   Have an excellent [[resume]].
    *   Be a strong overall candidate.
    *   Dress appropriately.
    *   Conduct thorough prep and research for the specific role/company.
    *   A strong referral can help open doors for the interview.
*   **The "Scarcity Principle" Application**:
    *   **Act as if you already have a job offer** from the company's competitor.
    *   **Act as if you have nine interviews scheduled** with other companies.
    *   This mindset makes you appear highly desirable, turning the tables where the company will want to convince *you* to join them.
    *   The moment you project this high-demand attitude, leveraging the psychological effect that "people want what they can't have," is when you win the interview.
*   **Negotiate Your Offer**:
    *   Once the job offer is extended, **always negotiate it**.
    *   A top candidate with many opportunities would naturally negotiate, reinforcing your perceived value and high demand.

#### Industry Trends:
*   The [[scarcity principle]] is a powerful psychological tool that can be strategically applied in job interviews to enhance a candidate's perceived value and leverage.
*   The job market often favors candidates who demonstrate strong self-worth and have multiple opportunities, leading to a more competitive and negotiation-driven hiring process for top talent.
