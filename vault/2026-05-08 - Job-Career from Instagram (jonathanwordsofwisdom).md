### Actionable Advice
Leverage [[Claude AI]] with these 4 [[prompts]] to achieve significant results in your [[job search]], potentially leading to a **[[90% resume score]]** and an average **[[10% interview rate]]**.

1.  **[[Resume Analysis & Job Recommendation]] (Prompt 1)**
    -   **Prompt**: "Act as a [[senior resume writer]]. Analyze my [[resume]] from top to bottom, identifying the top [[skills]] and [[experiences]], and sharing what [[jobs]] I should be applying for. For every [[job]] I should be applying for, give me the [[% interview rate]] and 5 real [[job links]] I can apply to that have been posted in the last 24 hours."
    -   **Outcome**: Get an analysis of your top [[skills]] and [[experiences]], recommended best-fit [[jobs]] based on your background, realistic [[interview rate]] estimates per [[role]], and 5 real [[job links]] posted recently for each.

2.  **[[Interview Likelihood & Resume Tweaks]] (Prompt 2)**
    -   **Prompt**: "Act as a [[recruiter]] hiring for a (ROLE YOU'RE INTERESTED IN) from this [[job description]] (**ATTACH AS [[PDF]]**). Identify if I would get an [[interview]] or not for this [[role]] with my [[resume]]. If yes, explain why, and if no, explain why not and how I could get an [[interview]]."
    -   **Outcome**: Receive an [[interview verdict]] (clear yes or no), honest [[recruiter-perspective feedback]], actionable steps to close gaps and boost chances, and specific [[resume tweaks]] to better match the [[role]].
    -   **[[Evaluation Criteria]]**: [[Skills & qualifications match]], [[relevant experience & impact]], [[keywords]] & [[ATS alignment]], overall fit for the [[role]] & [[company]].

3.  **[[Networking Strategist]] (Prompt 3)**
    -   **Prompt**: "Act as a [[networking strategist]] identifying [[recruiters]] and [[hiring managers]] for this [[job description]] (**ATTACH AS [[PDF]]**). Find 5 [[recruiters]], [[hiring managers]], or [[hiring team members]] that I could connect with on [[LinkedIn]] to reach out to. For each person, create a message that I should send them asking about the [[job opportunity]] and how my [[skills & experiences]] fit the [[role]]."
    -   **Outcome**: Get a list of people to connect with on [[LinkedIn]] (Name, Title, [[Company]]) and personalized [[message templates]] to send them.
        -   *Example Messages:*
            -   **To Recruiter**: "Hi [Name], I came across your profile while exploring opportunities at [[Company Name]]. I'm very interested in the {Role} position and believe my experience in {relevant skill or experience} aligns well with what you're looking for. Would love to learn more about the [[role]] and how I might be a fit. Thank you!"
            -   **To Hiring Manager**: "Hi [Name], I'm really impressed by the work your team is doing at [[Company Name]]. As someone with a background in {relevant experience}, I'm excited about the {Role} [[role]] and would love to connect to learn more about the team's goals and how I could contribute."

4.  **[[Resume & Cover Letter Generation]] (Prompt 4)**
    -   **Prompt**: "Act as a top 1% [[job-seeker]] with a [[99% interview rate]]. Create me a [[resume]] and [[cover letter]] that will get me multiple [[interviews]] for a (ROLE) with the following [[skills]]: Skill #1, Skill #2, Skill #3."
    -   **Outcome**: Generate a professional [[resume]] and [[cover letter]] tailored to a specific [[role]] and [[skills]].
    -   **[[Resume Template Highlights]]**:
        -   **[[Professional Summary]]**: Driver-role with proven success in leveraging [[Skill #1]], [[#2]], [[#3]] to drive measurable results.
        -   **[[Core Skills]]**: Explicitly list 3 [[key skills]].
        -   **[[Professional Experience]]**: [[Quantifiable achievements]] (e.g., "Achieved (X)% improvement in [key metric] by applying [[Skill #1]]").
    -   **[[Cover Letter Template Highlights]]**:
        -   Express strong interest in the (ROLE) position with [[expertise]] in [[Skill #1]], [[#2]], [[#3]].
        -   Connect current [[experience]] to the [[role]] (e.g., "Used [[Skill #1]] to [accomplishment + metric]").
        -   Emphasize [[cultural fit]] and [[contribution]].
