**15 Websites Everyone Should Know**

1.  **[[removepaywall.com]]**
    Read any article without [[paywalls]], no signups, no [[tracking]].

2.  **[[justtherecipe.com]]**
    Paste the [[URL]] to any [[recipe]], click submit, and it'll return literally JUST the [[recipe]], no [[ads]], no life story of the writer, no nothing EXCEPT the [[recipe]].

3.  **[[pdftolink.online]]**
    Turn any [[PDF]] into a [[link]] or [[QR code]] instantly.

4.  **[[tosdr.org]]**
    Summarizes [[terms of service]] and rates them for [[privacy]].

5.  **[[forvo.com]]**
    Learning a new [[language]]? Just type in a word and hear [[native speakers]] pronounce it. It's all user-submitted, and perfect for fixing your [[pronunciation]].

6.  **[[first2apply.com]]**
    Get notified the moment a [[job]] opens so you can apply before anyone else.

7.  **[[app.drawcharts.xyz]]**
    Create clean, simple [[charts]] with just plain [[text]]. No [[Excel]], just copy, paste, and visualize.

8.  **[[openculture.com]]**
    Free [[online courses]], [[books]], [[media]]...

9.  **[[cantsleepreads.com]]**
    A cool site for fellow [[insomniacs]] that gives you a random read for the night.

10. **[[musclewiki.com]]**
    Select a [[muscle]] and it provides you with [[exercises]] to [[workout]] the selected [[muscle]].

11. **[[onelook.com]]**
    It's a [[reverse dictionary]]. For when you can describe the thing but can't remember what it's called (and if you search "The inability to remember a word" you'll find the name for the struggle, [[lethologica]]).

12. **[[ihavenotv.com]]**
    Access to a curated list of >4000 [[documentaries]] that you can watch online for free.

13. **[[haveibeenpwned.com]]**
    Check if your [[passwords]] and other [[data]] leaked in any [[data breach]]. [[Have I Been Pwned]] checks if your [[email address]] is in a [[data breach]].

14. **[[weather.gov]]**
    None of annoying [[ads]] or [[storm stories]], just actual [[weather forecasts]] and hourly [[forecasts]] using [[charts]] instead of [[storm cloud graphics]].
