### Original Caption
* Save this recipe of easy Chicken Fajitas for your next lazy dinner night!🍗😋

Ingredients:
2 chicken breasts (thinly sliced)
1 red bell pepper
1 yellow bell pepper
1 onion
1 tbsp olive oil
Juice of ½ lime

Spices:
1 tsp paprika
1 tsp chili powder
½ tsp cumin
½ tsp garlic powder
½ tsp onion powder
½ tsp oregano
Salt & pepper

Method:
Toss chicken with oil, lime & spices.
Air fry at 375°F (190°C) for 10–12 mins.

In a hot pan, cook onions + peppers + same spices(optional) on high heat until slightly charred.
Add cooked chicken back in and toss for 30–45 seconds.
Finish with a squeeze of lime.

Warm tortillas, fill generously and enjoy.☺️
