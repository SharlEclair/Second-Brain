## Description
The way I’ve stopped making [[decisions]] alone

There’s a concept called the [[LLM Council]], originally from [[Andrej Karpathy]] and built out by [[Ole Lehmann]], and it’s changed how I [[reason]] through anything important and a big thank you to [[@aiwithremy]] for introducing me to it!

Instead of asking [[Claude]] what it thinks and taking that one answer at face value, you run your question through five completely different minds simultaneously!!!

Here’s what makes it actually useful: they don’t just give opinions. They review each other’s responses anonymously, poke holes in each other’s [[reasoning]], and then a [[chairman]] synthesises where they agreed, where they clashed, and what all of them missed.

And the thing I keep thinking about is that we’ve quietly stopped sitting with our [[decisions]]. The [[council]] forces you back into that. You have to frame the [[question]], which means you have to actually know what you’re asking. That alone is half the work.
