## [[Andrej Karpathy]]'s [[LLM]]-Powered [[Wiki System]]

[[Andrej Karpathy]]'s approach to [[knowledge management]] with [[LLMs]] eschews [[Retrieval Augmented Generation]] ([[RAG]]) in favor of a [[structured internal wiki]]. The core insight is that the [[context limit]] was never the problem, but rather the *[[structure]]* of the information. This system processes the data cost-effectively at the point of ingestion.

### [[Infrastructure]]: Three Folders, One File

His entire [[infrastructure]] consists of a single [[project folder]] containing three subfolders and one [[markdown file]]:

*   **`raw/`**: Contains your immutable [[source material]] (e.g., [[.md]], [[.txt files]]).
*   **`wiki/`**: This folder is entirely owned and written by the [[LLM]].
*   **`outputs/`**: Stores answers, reports, and analyses generated by the [[LLM]].
*   **`CLAUDE.md`**: A critical file that tells the [[LLM]] how to 'think' and operate.

### The [[Workflow]] in Seven Steps

1.  **Step 01: Setup the [[Project Folder]]**
    *   Create a [[project folder]].
    *   Inside, create three subfolders: `raw`, `wiki`, `outputs`.
    *   Create a text file `[[CLAUDE.md]]` in the root of the [[project folder]].
    *   **Roles**: `raw/` is immutable; `wiki/` is [[LLM]]-owned; `outputs/` stores answers.

2.  **Step 02: Write Your [[Schema]] in `[[CLAUDE.md]]`**
    *   Open `[[CLAUDE.md]]` and provide a [[schema]] for your [[LLM]]. This [[schema]] defines:
        *   What the [[wiki]] covers.
        *   [[Directory rules]].
        *   [[Ingest workflow]].
        *   [[Query workflow]].
    *   **Example Prompt for [[Schema]] Creation**: "I'm building an [[LLM wiki]] following [[Karpathy]]'s system. Help me write a [[CLAUDE.md]] schema for the topic: [your topic]. Include: what the [[wiki]] covers, [[directory rules]], [[ingest workflow]], and [[query workflow]]."

3.  **Step 03: Add [[Sources]] & [[Ingest]]**
    *   Add your [[source materials]] (preferably [[.md]] or [[.txt files]]) to the `raw/` folder.
    *   Run the following [[LLM prompt]] to process and [[ingest]] the data:
        ```
        Read everything in raw/.
        Compile wiki/ following CLAUDE.md rules.
        Create index.md first.
        Append to log.md when done.
        ```

4.  **Step 04: `[[Index.md]]` for Navigation**
    *   `[[Index.md]]` is created by the [[LLM]] during [[ingestion]] and acts as the [[LLM]]'s way to find information without a [[vector database]].
    *   It lists every [[wiki page]] with a link, a one-line summary, and optional [[metadata]].
    *   During [[query time]], the [[LLM]] reads `[[index.md]]` first to find relevant pages, pulls only those, and then generates answers.
    *   The [[LLM]] updates `[[index.md]]` automatically on every [[ingest]].

5.  **Step 05: `[[Log.md]]` for [[Session Memory]]**
    *   `[[Log.md]]` enables the [[LLM]] to remember across sessions, picking up where it left off.
    *   **[[Karpathy]]'s Format**: `## [YYYY-MM-DD] ingest | Article Title`
    *   This format makes the [[log]] parseable with simple terminal commands (e.g., `grep "^## \[" log.md | tail -5`).

6.  **Step 06: [[Compounding Knowledge]]**
    *   Answers generated by the [[LLM]] are filed back into the system, [[compounding its knowledge]].
    *   **Example Prompt**: "What are the 3 biggest gaps in my current research? Save the answer to outputs/ and file a summary into wiki/"
    *   **Tip**: Explicitly tell the [[LLM]] where to save answers to prevent them from disappearing into chat history.

7.  **Step 07: [[Periodic Health Checks]]**
    *   Regularly check the [[wiki]]'s health to prevent issues from [[compounding]].
    *   **Example [[Health Check Prompt]]**: "Health check the wiki. Find: [[contradictions]] between pages, [[orphan pages]] with no [[inbound links]], concepts missing their own page, claims a [[web search]] could update. Suggest 3 new articles to write."

### Summary

*   [[Andrej Karpathy]]'s [[LLM wiki system]] uses a simple [[file structure]] (`raw/`, `wiki/`, `outputs/`, `[[CLAUDE.md]]`) to manage [[knowledge]] without [[RAG]].
*   The `[[CLAUDE.md]]` file defines the [[LLM]]'s operational [[schema]], while `[[index.md]]` facilitates efficient [[information retrieval]] and `[[log.md]]` maintains [[session continuity]].
*   The [[workflow]] emphasizes [[structured ingestion]], [[active querying]], and [[continuous health checks]] to build a [[compounding knowledge base]].
